- **Serve:** `modal deploy slm_learning/modal_app/<rung>_serve.py` — operator may need to author per-rung serve scripts (reference slm_learning/modal_app/serve_vllm.py). See slm_learning/reports/research/hermes_eval_integration.md for Nous integration design notes.
